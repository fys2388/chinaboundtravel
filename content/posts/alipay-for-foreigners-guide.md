## Can Foreigners Use Alipay in China?

Yes. Alipay is the most widely used mobile payment app in China, and it now supports international visitors directly:

- **Foreign passports** can be used for identity verification
- **International Visa and Mastercard** can be linked for payments
- **QR-code payments** work at millions of merchants, including restaurants, shops, and metro gates
- **Tourist-friendly limits** apply to unverified or partially verified accounts, so verification is worth completing

Alipay is not the only option -- WeChat Pay offers similar coverage -- but Alipay's international onboarding has historically been the smoothest for travelers, which is why this guide focuses on it.

## What You Need Before Setting Up Alipay

Before you start, gather four things:

1. **Passport** -- the exact name on the passport must match the name you enter
2. **Phone number** -- a working number for SMS verification (international numbers are accepted for tourist accounts)
3. **International card** -- a Visa or Mastercard enabled for international use
4. **Internet connection** -- Alipay needs data to authorize payments; plan mobile data before you land (see Recommended Travel Preparation Tools below)

A stable connection is the piece most travelers overlook. Without data, the app cannot complete a payment, so setting up an eSIM or local SIM before arrival removes a major failure point.

## How to Set Up Alipay Step by Step

1. **Download the app** -- Alipay is available for iOS and Android; install it before your trip
2. **Create an account** -- register with your phone number and verify the SMS code
3. **Verify identity** -- select the international visitor option and submit your passport details
4. **Link a card** -- add your Visa or Mastercard and confirm the card is enabled for international payments
5. **Activate payment** -- once verified, you can scan QR codes at merchants and metro gates

Verification can take a few minutes. If it fails, see the Common Problems section below.

## Common Alipay Problems

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Verification failed | Passport name mismatch | Enter the name exactly as printed on the passport |
| Foreign card declined | Card not enabled for international use | Contact your bank before the trip |
| SMS not received | Number format or roaming issues | Check the country code and try again with a stable connection |
| Payment unavailable | Region or account status restrictions | Complete verification; confirm the merchant accepts QR payment |

If a problem persists, Alipay's in-app help center is the fastest source of current guidance.

## FAQ

### Can tourists use Alipay without a Chinese bank account?

Yes. International visitors can verify Alipay with their passport and link an international card. No Chinese bank account is required for daily payments.

### Can foreigners link Visa or Mastercard?

Yes, in most regions. Card eligibility can change, so check in the app before your trip and confirm your card is enabled for international use.

### Does Alipay work for subway payments?

Yes. QR-code scanning at metro gates works in most major cities, which makes Alipay practical for daily transport.

### Why is Alipay verification failing?

Usually a passport name mismatch, unsupported card, or region restriction. Re-check the name exactly as printed and confirm international card access with your bank.

### Do I need internet access to use Alipay?

Yes. Alipay needs a data connection at payment time, so arrange mobile data before arrival.
