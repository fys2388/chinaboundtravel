## Introduction

Yes — foreigners can set up and use Alipay in China in 2026. Download the app, register with your passport, link an international Visa or Mastercard (or a Chinese bank card for smoother service), and complete identity verification. Alipay is accepted at restaurants, shops, metro stations, taxis, and online services across China — cash is rarely needed in major cities. This complete guide covers the step-by-step setup process, linking payment methods, using QR codes for transactions, transfer limits, and common mistakes to avoid. If Alipay doesn't work for you, [WeChat Pay](/posts/wechat-pay-for-foreigners-step-by-step-setup-and-common-mistakes-to-avoid-guide/) is the other major option, and [PayPal now supports Alipay and WeChat Pay QR codes](/posts/paypal-alipay-wechat-pay-qr-code-support/) as a workaround.

## Why You Need Mobile Payment in China

Here's why mobile payment matters in China. Cash is becoming increasingly obsolete. Most businesses, big and small, prefer mobile payment. Whether you're buying a cup of coffee from a street vendor or checking out of a luxury hotel, Alipay is likely to be an accepted payment method.

Not only is it convenient, but it also offers a level of security. With Alipay, you don't have to carry around large amounts of cash, which reduces the risk of theft. Additionally, many stores offer discounts and promotions for customers who pay with Alipay.

Another reason is that it's easier to keep track of your expenses. Alipay provides detailed transaction records, so you can easily see where your money is going. This can be especially useful when you're on a budget.

## Part 1: Setting Up Alipay for Foreigners

### Downloading the App
The first step is to download the Alipay app. You can find it in the App Store for iOS or Google Play for Android. Once you've downloaded it, open the app and select "Sign Up." You'll be prompted to choose your country/region. As an American, you'll select the United States.

### Registering an Account
After choosing your country, you'll need to enter your phone number. Make sure you use a valid mobile number that you can access in China. You'll receive a verification code via text message. Enter the code to proceed.

Next, you'll be asked to set up a password. Choose a strong password that you can remember but is also difficult for others to guess.

### Linking a Payment Method
Now comes the important part-linking a payment method. As a foreigner, you have a few options. You can link your international credit card or use a service like PayPal.

If you choose to link your credit card, you'll need to enter your card details. Alipay supports most major international credit cards. Once you've entered the details, Alipay will verify the card.

If you want to use PayPal, you'll need to link your PayPal account to Alipay. To do this, open PayPal and link your US/foreign credit card or bank account as normal. Then, when you're in Alipay and want to add a payment method, select PayPal.

### Verifying Your Identity
In some cases, you may need to verify your identity further. This could involve uploading a copy of your passport or other identification documents. Alipay will guide you through the process.

## Part 2: Using Alipay for Transactions

### Scanning QR Codes
The most common way to use Alipay is by scanning QR codes. When you want to pay for something, the merchant will show you a QR code. Open your Alipay app and click on the "Scan" button. Point your phone's camera at the QR code, and Alipay will automatically detect it.

Once the QR code is scanned, you'll see the payment details on your screen. Review the amount and make sure it's correct. Then, click "Pay" and enter your password or use your fingerprint (if you've set up fingerprint payment).

### Paying in Stores
Many stores in China have Alipay terminals. You can simply tap your phone on the terminal or scan the QR code on the terminal with your Alipay app. Some stores also allow you to pay by showing your Alipay barcode.

### Online Shopping
Alipay is also widely used for online shopping in China. When you're checking out on an e-commerce website, look for the Alipay payment option. Click on it, and you'll be redirected to the Alipay app to complete the payment.

### Transferring Money
You can also use Alipay to transfer money to other Alipay users. To do this, click on the "Transfer" button in the Alipay app. Enter the recipient's phone number or Alipay username and the amount you want to transfer. Then, follow the prompts to complete the transfer.

## Part 3: Tips for Smooth Transactions

### Keep Your App Updated
Make sure you keep your Alipay app up-to - date. Updates often include security patches and new features that can improve your user experience.

### Be Careful with Your Password
Your Alipay password is your key to your digital wallet. Don't share it with anyone, and make sure you use a strong password. Consider using a password manager to keep your passwords secure.

### Check Your Transaction Records
Regularly check your Alipay transaction records to make sure there are no unauthorized charges. If you notice anything suspicious, contact Alipay customer service immediately.

### Know the Exchange Rates
If you're using an international credit card linked to Alipay, be aware of the exchange rates. Some credit card companies may charge additional fees for international transactions.

### Use Alipay in Safe Environments
Avoid using Alipay in public Wi-Fi networks that are not secure. Hackers could potentially intercept your payment information. Stick to using your mobile data or a trusted, password-protected Wi-Fi network.

## Part 4: Alternatives When Mobile Payment Isn't Working

### Cash
Although cash is becoming less common in China, it's still a good idea to carry some with you. In some small towns or rural areas, you may encounter businesses that don't accept mobile payment.

### Credit Cards
Some businesses may accept international credit cards directly. However, this is not as common as using Alipay. Make sure your credit card has a chip and PIN for added security.

### WeChat Pay
[WeChat Pay](/posts/wechat-pay-for-foreigners-step-by-step-setup-and-common-mistakes-to-avoid-guide/) is another popular mobile payment method in China. If Alipay isn't working for some reason, you may be able to use WeChat Pay instead. The setup process for WeChat Pay is similar to Alipay.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Can foreigners use Alipay in China without a Chinese bank account?

Yes. Foreigners can link an international Visa or Mastercard to Alipay's Tour Card feature, which is designed for short-term visitors. You don't need a Chinese bank account or Chinese phone number to register — your passport is sufficient for identity verification. However, a Chinese bank card provides higher transaction limits and smoother service for longer stays.

#### Which international cards are supported by Alipay?

Alipay supports Visa and Mastercard from major international banks, provided the card supports 3D Secure authentication. American Express and Discover may work in some cases but are less reliable. Smaller regional banks and prepaid cards often fail verification. If your card is declined, try a different issuer or use WeChat Pay as an alternative.

#### Are there transaction limits for foreign Alipay users?

Yes. Foreign users with the Tour Card have annual transaction limits (typically around 50,000 RMB per year) and per-transaction limits. These limits are sufficient for typical tourist expenses — meals, transport, shopping, and hotel stays. If you exceed the limit, you'll need to use a Chinese bank card or cash. Large purchases (over 5,000 RMB) may trigger additional identity verification.

#### How do I pay with Alipay at a store or restaurant?

There are two methods: (1) Scan the merchant's QR code — open Alipay, tap Scan, point at the code, enter the amount, and confirm. (2) Show your payment code — open Alipay, tap Pay, and let the merchant scan your QR code or barcode. Most large retailers use method 2, while small vendors and street stalls use method 1. Always confirm the amount before entering your payment password.

#### What should I do if my Alipay account gets restricted?

Account restrictions usually happen when Alipay's security system flags unusual activity — a new device, large transaction, or use in a different city. To resolve it, open Me > Settings > Account & Security and follow the identity verification prompts. You'll likely need to upload a photo of your passport. Keep a passport photo saved on your phone for quick re-verification. If the issue persists, contact Alipay customer service through the app's help center.
