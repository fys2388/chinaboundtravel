## 📋 What's Inside the Template

### 7-Day Classic China Route

| Days | City | Highlights | Pace |
|------|------|------------|------|
| 1–3 | **Beijing** | Great Wall (Mutianyu), Forbidden City, hutong exploration | Moderate — leave Day 3 flexible for jet lag |
| 4–5 | **Xi'an** | Terracotta Warriors (morning), Muslim Quarter (evening), City Wall cycling | Active — pack walking shoes |
| 6–7 | **Shanghai** | The Bund at night, French Concession cafes, Lujiazui skyline | Relaxed — perfect buffer before departure |

**Why this route works:** Beijing → Xi'an is a 4.5-hour high-speed train. Xi'an → Shanghai is a 5.5-hour train or a 2-hour flight. You cover three completely different facets of China (imperial history, ancient Silk Road, modern metropolis) without doing back-to-back long-haul flights.
