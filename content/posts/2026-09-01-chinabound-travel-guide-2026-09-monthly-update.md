### Practical Setup for September Travel

Before you fly, three things matter more than the guide itself:

- **Connectivity** - You will need a working internet connection within hours of landing. Alipay, WeChat Pay, ride-hailing, and most QR-code entry all depend on it. A pre-installed eSIM saves you the airport counter queue.
- **Travel insurance** - Most home-country health plans do not cover China, and emergency medical evacuation can run past $50,000. See our [full China travel insurance comparison](/posts/best-travel-insurance-china/) before you book.
- **Tours and tickets** - Popular sites in Chengdu, Zhangjiajie, and Beijing sell out weeks ahead in peak season. Booking through an aggregator keeps you in English with free cancellation.
