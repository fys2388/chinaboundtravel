# Budget Planning for China

Hey there, fellow travelers! I'm Joran, born and raised in sunny San Diego, California, but for the past decade, I've called Chengdu, China, home.

## Why Budgeting in China Feels Different

China is not a monolithic "cheap" destination anymore. The trick is knowing where to spend and where to save.

## Accommodation Tips

Consider homestays via platforms like Xiaozhu. Budget doesn't have to mean miserable.

## Food and Transport

Street food is your best friend. High-speed trains are incredible but book ahead.

## Conclusion

Plan a rough budget, but leave room for spontaneity. Safe travels!
