Guilin is where China's most iconic scenery comes to life. The karst mountains rising from the Li River have inspired poets and painters for centuries—and once you see them in person, you'll understand why. This is not just a travel destination; it's a living postcard.

The phrase "Guilin's scenery is the best under heaven" (桂林山水甲天下) has been used for over 800 years, and it still holds true. From the mist-shrouded peaks of the Li River to the emerald rice terraces of Longsheng, from the ancient villages of Yangshuo to the otherworldly caves beneath the city, Guilin offers some of the most spectacular natural scenery on Earth.

## Best Time to Visit Guilin

Guilin has a subtropical monsoon climate with mild winters and hot, humid summers:

- **September to November (Autumn):** **Best time.** Cool weather (15-25°C/59-77°F), clear skies, and the Longsheng rice terraces turn golden in October. Less rain than summer.
- **March to May (Spring):** Good time. Mild temperatures (15-25°C/59-77°F), blooming flowers, and misty mountains. April and May can be rainy.
- **June to August (Summer):** Hot (28-35°C/82-95°F) and humid with frequent rain. The landscape is lush and green, and the Li River water levels are high. Thunderstorms are common.
- **December to February (Winter):** Cold (5-12°C/41-54°F) and damp, but less crowded. Misty mountains have a mystical, ink-painting feel. Hotel prices are lowest.

**Avoid:** Chinese New Year (late January/early February), the first week of October (National Day holiday), and mid-June to mid-July (plum rain season with constant drizzle).

## Getting There & Around

### Airport

- **KWL (Guilin Liangjiang International Airport):** 28km southwest of downtown.
  - **Airport Bus:** Multiple routes to downtown hotels and train stations. ¥20-30, 40-60 minutes.
  - **Taxi/Didi:** ¥80-120 to downtown, 30-45 minutes.

### High-Speed Rail

Guilin is connected to the national high-speed rail network:
- **Guilin North Station (桂林北):** Main high-speed rail station, 10km north of downtown.
  - To Guangzhou: 2.5-3 hours, ¥175-500
  - To Changsha: 3-4 hours, ¥200-600
  - To Guiyang: 2.5-3 hours, ¥150-450
  - To Nanning: 2.5-3 hours, ¥130-400
- **Guilin Station (桂林):** Older station, closer to downtown. Some high-speed and regular trains.

### Within the City

- **Buses:** Cheap (¥1-2) and cover most areas, but can be crowded and slow.
- **Taxis/Didi:** Affordable. A typical cross-city ride costs ¥20-40.
- **Electric bikes:** Great for exploring the surrounding areas and villages. Rent for ¥30-50 per day.
- **Bicycles:** Perfect for Yangshuo and the countryside. Rent for ¥10-20 per day.

### Li River Cruise

The most popular way to see Guilin's scenery. A 4-hour cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo through the most spectacular karst landscape.

- **Tickets:** ¥215-360 depending on class (economy, deluxe, luxury). Book in advance, especially in peak season.
- **Departure:** Zhujiang Pier (竹江码头) or Mopanshan Pier (磨盘山码头), 30-40 minutes from downtown.
- **Duration:** 4-5 hours, arrives in Yangshuo around 2-3 PM.
- **Best time:** Early morning for misty views, or late afternoon for golden light.
- **Pro tip:** Sit on the open-air upper deck for the best views. Bring sunscreen and a hat.

## Must-See Sights

### Li River (漓江)

The star attraction. The Li River winds 83km from Guilin to Yangshuo through the most spectacular karst landscape you'll ever see. The scenery changes every minute—limestone peaks rising from emerald water, ancient villages, bamboo groves, and fishermen with their cormorants.

- **How to experience:**
  - **Cruise:** The classic 4-hour boat ride from Guilin to Yangshuo. Most popular option.
  - **Bamboo raft:** Smaller, more intimate rafts (actually motorized PVC boats) from Yangshuo to Xingping. ¥120-200 per person.
  - **Hiking:** The Li River hiking trail from Yangdi to Xingping (15km, 4-5 hours) is one of China's most scenic hikes.
  - **Cycling:** Follow the river from Guilin to Yangshuo, stopping at villages and viewpoints.
- **Best viewpoints:**
  - **Xingping (兴坪):** The most famous section—this is the scenery on the ¥20 banknote.
  - **Huangbu (黄布):** "Yellow Cloth" reflection, where the mountains mirror perfectly in the still water.
  - **Mural Hill (画山):** A 400m cliff with natural patterns that look like nine horses.
- **Pro tip:** The Li River is best seen in the early morning when mist hangs over the water and the mountains. Avoid midday when the sun is harsh and the crowds are largest.

### Elephant Trunk Hill (象鼻山)

Guilin's most famous landmark—a mountain shaped like an elephant drinking from the Li River. The "elephant" is 55m long, 25m wide, and 25m high, with a natural arch between its trunk and body.

- **Tickets:** ¥55.
- **When to go:** Early morning or late afternoon for good light. The park is small, allow 1-2 hours.
- **What to do:**
  - Climb to the top of the hill for panoramic views of Guilin and the Li River.
  - Walk through the "Water Moon Cave" (水月洞), the natural arch between the trunk and body.
  - Visit the Puxian Pagoda on top of the hill.
  - Take photos from the opposite bank for the classic "elephant drinking" view.
- **Pro tip:** The hill is located in a park right in the city center. Combine with a walk along the Li River or a visit to nearby Fubo Hill.

### Reed Flute Cave (芦笛岩)

A massive limestone cave with colorful lighting. The stalactites and stalagmites are over 700,000 years old, and the cave has been a tourist attraction for over 1,200 years (ancient graffiti dating back to 792 AD has been found inside).

- **Tickets:** ¥90.
- **When to go:** Any time—the cave is underground and temperature-controlled (around 18°C/64°F year-round). Allow 1-1.5 hours.
- **What to see:**
  - The cave is 240m long with a 500m walking path.
  - Colorful lighting illuminates the formations, which have poetic names like "Crystal Palace," "Mushroom Hill," and "Virgin Forest."
  - The largest chamber can hold 1,000 people.
- **Pro tip:** The cave can be slippery—wear shoes with good grip. The lighting is designed for photos, so bring a camera. The cave is a good option on rainy days.

### Fubo Hill (伏波山)

A hill overlooking the Li River with ancient caves and temples. The "Wave-Subduing Rock" (还珠洞) is a natural arch that's become a symbol of Guilin.

- **Tickets:** ¥30.
- **When to go:** Morning or late afternoon. Allow 1 hour.
- **What to see:**
  - Climb to the top for views of the Li River and city.
  - Visit the Thousand Buddha Cave (千佛洞) with ancient Buddhist carvings.
  - See the "Sword Testing Rock" (试剑石), a stalactite that almost touches the ground.
- **Pro tip:** Fubo Hill is smaller and less crowded than Elephant Trunk Hill. Combine with a visit to Diecai Hill (叠彩山) nearby for more views.

### Seven Star Park (七星公园)

The largest park in Guilin (120 hectares), with beautiful gardens, lakes, karst peaks, and a zoo. Don't miss the Camel Mountain (骆驼山), which looks exactly like a sitting camel.

- **Tickets:** ¥55 (includes the zoo and Seven Star Cave).
- **When to go:** Morning. Allow 2-3 hours.
- **What to see:**
  - Camel Mountain (骆驼山): The most famous rock formation in the park.
  - Seven Star Cave (七星岩): Another limestone cave with colorful lighting.
  - Flower Bridge (花桥): A beautiful ancient bridge over the river.
  - The zoo: Has pandas, monkeys, and other animals.
- **Pro tip:** The park is large and can be tiring in the heat. Rent an electric cart (¥30) to get around. The park is popular with locals for morning exercise.

### Two Rivers and Four Lakes (两江四湖)

A scenic waterway system connecting the Li River, Taohua River, and four lakes (Rong, Shan, Gui, and Mulong) in downtown Guilin. The night cruise is especially beautiful with illuminated bridges and pagodas.

- **Tickets:** ¥185 (night cruise), ¥90 (day cruise).
- **Duration:** 90 minutes.
- **When to go:** Night cruise (7:30-9:30 PM) for the best lighting and atmosphere.
- **Pro tip:** You can also walk along the lakes for free. The Sun and Moon Twin Pagodas (日月双塔) at Shan Lake are especially beautiful at night.

## Day Trips from Guilin

### Yangshuo (阳朔)

Just an hour by bus from Guilin, but feels like a different world. Bicycle rides along the Yulong River, bamboo rafting, ancient villages, and the famous West Street.

- **How to get there:** Bus from Guilin South Bus Station (¥25, 1.5 hours) or high-speed train to Yangshuo Station (¥20, 30 minutes, then bus to town).
- **What to do:**
  - **Cycle the Yulong River valley:** Rent a bike (¥10-20/day) and ride through rice fields, karst peaks, and ancient villages. One of China's best cycling experiences.
  - **Bamboo raft on the Yulong River:** ¥160-240 per raft (2 people), 1-2 hours. More peaceful than the Li River.
  - **West Street (西街):** The famous tourist street with bars, restaurants, and shops. Lively at night.
  - **Impression Sanjie Liu (印象刘三姐):** A massive outdoor light show on the Li River, directed by Zhang Yimou. ¥200-600.
  - **Moon Hill (月亮山):** A hill with a natural arch shaped like a crescent moon. Climb to the top for views (¥15).
- **Stay:** Many travelers spend 2-3 nights in Yangshuo instead of Guilin.

### Longsheng Rice Terraces (龙脊梯田)

A 2-hour drive from Guilin. The terraces are carved into the mountainside by the Zhuang and Yao ethnic minorities over 650 years ago. Especially beautiful in June (green rice) and October (golden harvest).

- **Tickets:** ¥80.
- **How to get there:** Bus from Guilin (¥30, 2.5 hours) or private car/driver (¥300-400 round trip).
- **What to see:**
  - **Ping'an Village (平安寨):** The most developed area, with the famous "Seven Stars Around the Moon" and "Nine Dragons and Five Tigers" viewpoints.
  - **Jinkeng Village (金坑):** Less developed, more authentic, with the "Thousand Layers to the Sky" viewpoint. Cable car available (¥110 round trip).
  - **Huangluo Yao Village (黄洛瑶寨):** Known for the women with extremely long hair (average 1.5m).
- **Best time:** Mid-June (water-filled terraces, green), early October (golden harvest), or winter (snow-covered, rare but beautiful).
- **Stay:** Spend a night in a traditional wooden guesthouse in one of the villages. ¥100-200/night.

### Xingping Ancient Town (兴坪古镇)

A 1.5-hour drive from Guilin (or 30 minutes from Yangshuo). This ancient town on the Li River is the setting for the scenery on the ¥20 banknote.

- **Tickets:** Free to enter the town.
- **What to do:**
  - Walk the ancient streets with Ming and Qing dynasty buildings.
  - Take photos of the ¥20 banknote scenery from the riverbank.
  - Visit the old fishing harbor and see cormorant fishermen.
  - Take a bamboo raft on the Li River.
- **Pro tip:** Xingping is much quieter and less touristy than Yangshuo. Great for a half-day trip or an overnight stay.

## Where to Eat

Guilin has its own unique cuisine, different from the spicy Sichuan or Cantonese styles. The flavors are subtle, fresh, and often sour.

### Signature Dishes

- **Guilin Rice Noodles (桂林米粉):** The city's signature dish. Fresh rice noodles in a savory broth with toppings like braised pork, beef, peanuts, scallions, and pickled vegetables. Eat "dry" (捞粉) or with soup (汤粉). ¥8-15 at any local shop.
  - **Where:** Chongshan Road (崇善路), Shitou Road (石涛路), or any street stall with a line of locals.
- **Beer Fish (啤酒鱼):** Fresh fish (usually carp or catfish) cooked with beer, chili peppers, tomatoes, and garlic. Popular in Yangshuo as well. ¥60-100 per fish.
  - **Where:** Yangshuo West Street area, or any restaurant along the Li River.
- **Bamboo Shoot Soup (竹笋汤):** Fresh bamboo shoots from the surrounding mountains, served in a light broth with pork or chicken. Sweet and tender. ¥20-40.
- **Osmanthus Cake (桂花糕):** A sweet snack made with osmanthus flowers, glutinous rice, and sugar. Soft, fragrant, and not too sweet. ¥5-10 from street vendors.
- **Stuffed Li River Snails (漓江田螺):** Large snails from the Li River stuffed with minced pork, mint, and spices. A local specialty. ¥20-30.
- **Oil Tea (油茶):** A bitter, savory tea made by pounding tea leaves with ginger, garlic, and peanuts, then adding hot water. Served with puffed rice, peanuts, and snacks. An acquired taste, popular with the Zhuang and Yao minorities. ¥10-20.

### Restaurants

- **Chongshan Rice Noodles (崇善米粉):** Famous local chain for Guilin rice noodles. Multiple locations. ¥10-20 per person.
- **Shi Wai Tao Yuan (世外桃源):** Restaurant in a beautiful garden setting, serving traditional Guilin cuisine. ¥80-150 per person.
- **Lazy Cat's Kitchen (懒猫厨房):** Popular in Yangshuo, serving both Chinese and Western food. Great for travelers. ¥50-100 per person.
- **Yangshuo Beer Fish Master (阳朔啤酒鱼大师):** Famous for beer fish in Yangshuo. ¥80-120 per person.

### Street Food

- **Zhengyang Pedestrian Street (正阳步行街):** Downtown Guilin's main shopping and food street.
- **Shangshuijing Lane (尚水巷):** A narrow alley near Zhengyang Street with lots of local snack stalls.
- **Yangshuo West Street (西街):** The most famous tourist street, with bars, restaurants, and street food.

## Where to Stay

### Downtown Guilin (秀峰区/象山区)
- **Best for:** First-time visitors, convenience, city attractions.
- **Price range:** ¥250-1000/night.
- **Pros:** Close to Elephant Trunk Hill, Two Rivers and Four Lakes, restaurants, and shopping. Good transport links.
- **Cons:** City center can be noisy. Less scenic than Yangshuo.

### Along the Li River (七星区)
- **Best for:** Scenery, relaxation, riverside views.
- **Price range:** ¥400-1500/night.
- **Pros:** Beautiful river views, close to Seven Star Park, quieter.
- **Cons:** Farther from the main dining and shopping areas.

### Yangshuo (阳朔县)
- **Best for:** Nature, cycling, countryside, longer stays.
- **Price range:** ¥150-1000/night.
- **Pros:** The best scenery in the region, great for cycling and hiking, relaxed atmosphere.
- **Cons:** 1-1.5 hours from Guilin city. West Street can be noisy at night.

### Budget Options
- **Guilin Central Youth Hostel:** Near Zhengyang Street, clean and social. ¥60-120/night for a dorm bed.
- **Yangshuo Showbiz Hostel:** Near West Street, popular with backpackers. ¥50-100/night.

### Day 1: Guilin City & Li River
- **8:00 AM:** Elephant Trunk Hill (1-2 hours)
- **10:00 AM:** Fubo Hill + Diecai Hill (1-2 hours)
- **12:00 PM:** Lunch at a local restaurant (try Guilin rice noodles)
- **1:30 PM:** Reed Flute Cave (1-1.5 hours)

- **6:00 PM:** Dinner downtown
- **7:30 PM:** Two Rivers and Four Lakes night cruise (90 minutes) OR walk along the lakes to see the illuminated pagodas

### Day 2: Li River Cruise & Yangshuo
- **7:30 AM:** Transfer to Zhujiang/Mopanshan Pier
- **9:00 AM - 1:30 PM:** Li River cruise to Yangshuo (4-5 hours, lunch on board)
- **2:00 PM:** Arrive in Yangshuo. Rent a bike (¥10-20)
- **2:30 - 5:30 PM:** Cycle the Yulong River valley (rice fields, karst peaks, ancient villages)
- **6:00 PM:** Return bike, check into hotel or prepare to return to Guilin
- **7:00 PM:** Dinner in Yangshuo (try beer fish)
- **8:30 PM:** West Street nightlife OR return to Guilin by bus (last bus around 9 PM)

## Practical Tips

### Money & Payments
- Alipay and WeChat Pay are accepted everywhere, including most restaurants, shops, and ticket counters.
- Carry ¥200-500 cash for small vendors, villages, and emergencies.
- ATMs are available in the city, but may be limited in rural areas like Longsheng.

### Internet & VPN
- Google, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube are blocked.
- **Set up a VPN before arrival.** ExpressVPN, Astrill, and NordVPN work well.
- 4G/5G coverage is good in the city and along the Li River, but may be spotty in mountainous areas like Longsheng.

### Language
- English is not widely spoken outside major hotels and tourist areas.
- Download Pleco (Chinese dictionary) and Google Translate (offline pack) for menus and directions.
- In rural areas and ethnic minority villages, even Mandarin may not be universally spoken—patience and gestures go a long way.

### Safety
- Guilin is very safe. Violent crime is rare.
- Watch for pickpockets in crowded areas (bus stations, West Street, tourist sites).
- Be careful when hiking or cycling—some trails can be slippery, especially in the rain.
- Use licensed tour operators and official ticket counters. Avoid "free tours" that lead to shopping scams.
- The Li River can have strong currents—swimming is not recommended.

### Health
- Guilin is humid—stay hydrated and use mosquito repellent, especially near the river and in rural areas.
- The food is generally safe—stick to busy restaurants with high turnover.
- Drink bottled or boiled water only.
- Bring sunscreen and a hat—the sun can be strong even on cloudy days.
- If hiking in Longsheng, bring proper shoes and plenty of water.

### Tipping
- Tipping is not customary. No need to tip at restaurants, for taxis, or for guides.
- Some high-end hotels and tour guides may appreciate a small tip, but it's not expected.

### What to Pack
- Comfortable walking shoes (you'll walk a lot, especially on uneven terrain)
- Light, breathable clothing (humid climate)
- Rain jacket or umbrella (frequent rain, especially in spring and summer)
- Sunscreen, hat, and sunglasses
- Portable power bank (essential for navigation and payments)
- Universal adapter (Type A, C, I, 220V)
- Mosquito repellent
- Toilet paper (many public restrooms don't provide it)
- Hand sanitizer
- Swimsuit (if your hotel has a pool, or for hot springs)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a visa to visit Guilin?
Most nationalities need a visa to visit China. However, citizens of 55+ countries can use the **144-hour visa-free transit policy** when transiting through Guilin to a third country. Check our [complete China visa guide](/posts/ultimate-guide-to-china-visa-for-tourists/) for details.

### How many days do I need in Guilin?
2 days is the minimum to see the highlights (city sights + Li River cruise to Yangshuo). 3-4 days allows for day trips to Longsheng rice terraces, deeper exploration of Yangshuo, or a visit to Xingping. Many travelers spend 2 nights in Guilin and 2 nights in Yangshuo.

### Should I stay in Guilin or Yangshuo?
- **Stay in Guilin** if: You want easy access to city attractions (Reed Flute Cave, Elephant Trunk Hill), good transport links, and more restaurant/shopping options.
- **Stay in Yangshuo** if: You want the best scenery, love cycling/hiking, prefer a relaxed countryside atmosphere, and plan to spend most of your time outdoors.
- **Best of both:** Spend 1-2 nights in Guilin, then 2-3 nights in Yangshuo.

### Is the Li River cruise worth it?
Yes, absolutely. The Li River cruise is the classic Guilin experience and one of the most scenic boat rides in the world. The 4-5 hour cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo takes you through the most spectacular karst landscape. If you're short on time or budget, you can also do a shorter bamboo raft ride from Yangshuo to Xingping, which covers the most famous section.

### What's the best way to see the Longsheng Rice Terraces?
- **Best time:** Mid-June (water-filled, green) or early October (golden harvest).
- **How to get there:** Bus from Guilin (¥30, 2.5 hours) or private car (¥300-400 round trip).
- **How long:** A day trip is possible (leave early, return late), but spending a night in a village guesthouse is more relaxing and allows for sunrise/sunset views.
- **Which village:** Ping'an is more developed and easier; Jinkeng is more authentic and has a cable car.

### Can I cycle from Guilin to Yangshuo?
Yes, but it's a long ride (60-70km, 4-6 hours) and parts of the route are on busy roads. Most people prefer to:
1. Take the Li River cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo, then cycle around Yangshuo.
2. Take a bus to Yangshuo, then cycle the Yulong River valley (much more scenic, less traffic).
3. Do a guided cycling tour from Guilin to Yangshuo with support vehicle.

### Is Guilin safe for solo travelers?
Yes, Guilin is very safe for solo travelers, including women. The area is tourist-friendly and violent crime is rare. Use common sense with pickpockets in crowded areas, be careful when hiking/cycling, and use licensed operators. The locals are generally friendly and helpful.

### What should I eat in Guilin?
Must-try dishes: Guilin rice noodles (桂林米粉), beer fish (啤酒鱼), bamboo shoot soup, osmanthus cake, stuffed Li River snails, and oil tea (油茶). The rice noodles are the standout—try them at a local shop for breakfast, and you'll understand why they're the city's signature dish.

### Can I visit Guilin as a day trip from elsewhere?
Guilin is best experienced over 2-3 days, but if you're short on time:
- From Guangzhou: High-speed train takes 2.5-3 hours. A weekend trip (2 days) is feasible.
- From Changsha: 3-4 hours by high-speed train.
- From Guiyang: 2.5-3 hours by high-speed train.
- The Li River cruise alone takes a full day, so plan accordingly.

### Is Guilin worth visiting?
Absolutely. Guilin offers some of the most spectacular natural scenery on Earth. The karst mountains rising from the Li River are unlike anything else in the world, and the region's combination of natural beauty, ethnic minority culture, and outdoor activities makes it one of China's top travel destinations. If you enjoy nature, hiking, cycling, or photography, Guilin is a must-visit.
