## Top-rated China Itineraries for 10 Days: Three Routes Compared
## Introduction
China, a land of contrasts and wonders, offers a plethora of experiences for travelers. If you're planning a 10-day trip from Australia or New Zealand, you're in for a treat. In this guide, I'll compare three amazing routes that will take you through some of China's most iconic cities and landscapes.

## Route 1: Beijing, Xi'an, and Shanghai

### Beijing: The Capital's Majesty
Beijing, the capital city, is a blend of ancient and modern. The Forbidden City, a UNESCO World Heritage site, is a must-visit. With its red walls and golden roofs, it's like stepping back in time to the days of the emperors. The Temple of Heaven, another historical gem, is where emperors used to perform sacrifices and pray for good harvests. And of course, no trip to Beijing is complete without a visit to the Great Wall. There are several sections to choose from, each with its own charm.

Actionable tip: To avoid the crowds at the Forbidden City, arrive early in the morning. It's like getting a private tour of the imperial palace before the masses arrive.

### Xi'an: The Cradle of Chinese Civilization
Xi'an is famous for the Terracotta Army, an army of life-sized clay sculptures that were buried with Emperor Qin Shi Huang. It's an archaeological wonder that will leave you in awe. The city also has a rich history of silk production, and you can visit the Shaanxi History Museum to learn more about it. And don't forget to try some local specialties like roujiamo (Chinese hamburger) and yangrou paomo (pita bread soaked in lamb soup).

Actionable tip: When visiting the Terracotta Army, consider hiring a local guide. They can provide you with in-depth knowledge and interesting stories that you might miss on your own.

### Shanghai: The Modern Metropolis
Shanghai is a city that never sleeps. The Bund, with its colonial-era buildings and modern skyscrapers, is a symbol of the city's past and present. The Oriental Pearl Tower offers stunning views of the cityscape. And if you're a foodie, you have to try xiaolongbao (steamed dumplings) and other local delicacies.

Actionable tip: Take a night cruise on the Huangpu River to see the city's illuminated skyline. It's like a scene out of a science fiction movie.

## Route 2: Chengdu, Zhangjiajie, and Hong Kong

### Chengdu: The Panda Capital
Chengdu is home to the famous Chengdu Panda Base, where you can see pandas up close. It's a magical experience that will make you fall in love with these cute creatures. The city is also known for its delicious food, especially Sichuan hotpot. With its spicy and numbing flavors, it's a taste sensation.

Actionable tip: Go to the panda base early in the morning to see the pandas when they are most active. And make sure to book your tickets in advance.

### Zhangjiajie: The Avatar Mountains
Zhangjiajie is famous for its unique sandstone pillars, which were the inspiration for the floating mountains in the movie Avatar. The Zhangjiajie National Forest Park is a nature lover's paradise, with hiking trails and stunning views. You can also take a cable car or a glass-bottomed bridge for a different perspective.

Actionable tip: Bring comfortable hiking shoes and a camera to capture the breathtaking scenery. And be prepared for the crowds, especially during peak season.

### Hong Kong: The Pearl of the Orient
Hong Kong is a vibrant city with a rich history and culture. The Victoria Harbour is a must-visit, with its beautiful skyline and ferries. The city is also known for its shopping, from high-end luxury brands to street markets. And don't forget to try some local specialties like dim sum and egg tarts.

Actionable tip: Take a trip to the Peak Tram for panoramic views of the city. And explore the local neighborhoods like Central and Sheung Wan to discover hidden gems.

## Route 3: Hangzhou, Guilin, and Yangshuo

### Hangzhou: The City of Heaven
Hangzhou is famous for its beautiful West Lake, which is surrounded by temples, pagodas, and gardens. It's a peaceful and scenic place to relax and enjoy nature. The city is also known for its silk production and tea culture. You can visit a silk factory and a tea plantation to learn more about these traditions.

Actionable tip: Rent a bike and ride around the West Lake. It's a great way to explore the area and get some exercise at the same time.

### Guilin: The Land of Beauty
Guilin is known for its unique karst landscape, with its limestone peaks and clear rivers. The Li River is a popular destination for boat rides, where you can enjoy the beautiful scenery along the way. The city is also home to some historical sites like the Reed Flute Cave.

Actionable tip: Take an early morning boat ride on the Li River to see the sunrise over the mountains. It's a magical experience that you'll never forget.

### Yangshuo: The Outdoor Paradise
Yangshuo is a small town near Guilin that is famous for its outdoor activities. You can go rock climbing, hiking, or bamboo rafting. The town also has a laid-back atmosphere and is a great place to relax and enjoy the scenery.

Actionable tip: Hire a local guide to take you on a bamboo rafting trip down the Yulong River. It's a peaceful and scenic way to experience the area.
