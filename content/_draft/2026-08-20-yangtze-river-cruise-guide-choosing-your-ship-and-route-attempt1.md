## Choosing Your Ship
### Types of Ships
When it comes to Yangtze River cruises, there are several types of ships to choose from. The most common types are:
- **Luxury Cruise Ships:** These ships offer the highest level of comfort and amenities, with spacious cabins, gourmet dining, and a variety of onboard activities.
- **Mid-Range Cruise Ships:** These ships offer a good balance of comfort and affordability, with comfortable cabins, decent dining options, and a few onboard activities.
- **Budget Cruise Ships:** These ships are the most affordable option, but they may not offer the same level of comfort and amenities as the other types of ships.

### Ship Size
Another important factor to consider when choosing a ship is the size of the ship. The size of the ship will affect the level of comfort and amenities available, as well as the number of passengers on board.
- **Large Ships:** These ships can accommodate up to several thousand passengers and offer a wide range of amenities, such as multiple restaurants, bars, lounges, and entertainment venues.
- **Small Ships:** These ships can accommodate up to a few hundred passengers and offer a more intimate and personalized cruising experience.
