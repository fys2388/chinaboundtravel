## Kung Fu and Martial Arts in China: Where to See and Train
## Introduction
If you're a European traveler with a passion for martial arts, China is the ultimate destination. The land of Kung Fu offers a rich tapestry of traditions, ancient temples, and world-class training facilities. In this guide, I'll take you on a journey through a top choice places to witness the magic of Kung Fu and even get a chance to train like a master.

## The Cradle of Kung Fu-Henan Province

### Shaolin Temple
The Shaolin Temple is perhaps the most famous martial arts destination in the world. Located in Dengfeng, Henan Province, it has been a center of Buddhist learning and martial arts for over 1,500 years.

**History and Significance**:
The temple was founded in 495 AD and is known for its unique blend of Buddhism and martial arts. The Shaolin monks developed Kung Fu as a form of self-defense and a way to discipline the body and mind. Over the centuries, Shaolin Kung Fu has spread around the globe, influencing countless martial artists. It's like the "matrix" of martial arts, where the code of combat was born and has been evolving ever since.

**What to See**:
- **The Pagoda Forest**: This is a collection of over 240 pagodas that house the remains of Shaolin monks. It's a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a beautiful sight to behold. The pagodas stand tall and proud, like silent sentinels of the temple's long history. Each one has its own story, etched in the stones over time.
- **The Main Temple Buildings**: These are filled with ancient artifacts, murals, and statues that tell the story of the temple's history. As you walk through these buildings, it's as if you're穿越时空 (traveling through time). The murals depict scenes from the monks' lives, their training, and their spiritual journey.
- **Kung Fu Performances**: Every day, there are several Kung Fu shows where you can watch the monks demonstrate their incredible skills. The performances are a display of power, grace, and precision. It's like watching a live action movie, where the monks are the heroes, and their Kung Fu is the special effect.

**Training Opportunities**:
If you're interested in training at the Shaolin Temple, there are several schools in the area. However, it's important to note that training here is intense and requires a high level of commitment. You'll be learning from some of the most experienced Kung Fu masters in the world. But be warned, it's not for the faint of heart. It's like signing up for a boot camp, but instead of guns, you'll be using your fists and feet.

### Songshan Shaolin Martial Arts School
This is one of the largest and most well-known martial arts schools in the area. It offers a range of courses for beginners and advanced students alike.

**Courses Offered**:
- **Basic Kung Fu Training**: This includes fundamental movements. Here, you'll start from the very basics, like learning to stand correctly, which is the foundation of all Kung Fu stances. It's like building a house, you need a strong foundation to build on.
- **Advanced Techniques**: For those with some experience, the school offers advanced techniques training. This is where you'll start to learn more complex moves and combinations. It's like taking your driving skills to the next level, from basic driving to advanced maneuvers.
- **Weapons Training**: You can also learn to use traditional Chinese weapons such as the staff, sword, and spear. Handling these weapons is not only a great way to enhance your martial arts skills but also a fascinating insight into Chinese history and culture. It's like stepping into the shoes of a ancient warrior.

**Training Schedule**:
The school follows a strict training schedule. Classes usually start early in the morning and continue throughout the day, with breaks for meals and rest. It's important to be prepared for a full-day of intense physical activity. Think of it as a daily adventure, where you'll be pushed to your limits and beyond.

**Accommodation**:
The school provides accommodation for students. The rooms are simple but clean and comfortable. Staying on campus allows you to fully immerse yourself in the training environment. It's like living in a martial arts-themed dormitory.

## Other Martial Arts Destinations in China

### Wudang Mountains-Hubei Province
The Wudang Mountains are another famous martial arts destination in China. Known for its Taoist temples and the Wudang Kung Fu style.

**History and Style**:
The Wudang Kung Fu has a long history and is characterized by its soft and internal techniques. It emphasizes the cultivation of qi (energy) and the harmony of the body and mind. It's like a gentle dance of power and grace.

**What to See**:
- **The Forbidden City of the Wudang Mountains**: This is a magnificent complex of Taoist temples and palaces. It's a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a must-see for any visitor. The architecture is simply stunning, with its red walls and golden roofs.
- **Taijiquan Performances**: You can watch performances of Taijiquan, a form of Wudang Kung Fu. The slow and flowing movements are both beautiful and mesmerizing. It's like watching a meditation in motion.

**Training Opportunities**:
There are also training opportunities at the Wudang Mountains. You can learn Taijiquan and other Wudang Kung Fu styles from experienced masters. Training here is a great way to experience the unique philosophy and techniques of Wudang Kung Fu. It's like embarking on a spiritual journey through martial arts.

### Fujian Province-Southern Kung Fu
Fujian Province is known for its southern Kung Fu styles, such as Fujian White Crane and Five Ancestors.

**Styles and Characteristics**:
Southern Kung Fu styles are characterized by their short and powerful movements, as well as their emphasis on close-range combat. The styles often incorporate elements of animal movements, giving them a unique and distinct flavor. It's like watching a battle between different animal spirits.

**What to See**:
- **Kaiyuan Temple**: This temple in Quanzhou is a famous martial arts site. It has a long history of martial arts practice and is home to many martial arts masters. The temple itself is a beautiful example of traditional Chinese architecture.
- **Martial Arts Museums**: There are several martial arts museums in Fujian Province that showcase the history and culture of southern Kung Fu. These museums are a great place to learn about the different styles and their development over time. It's like stepping into a time machine and exploring the past of martial arts.

**Training Opportunities**:
You can find training classes and schools in Fujian Province that offer instruction in southern Kung Fu styles. Training here is a great way to experience the unique characteristics of southern Kung Fu and to learn from local masters. It's like joining a secret club of martial arts enthusiasts.

## Tips for Training in China

### Preparation
Before you start training, it's important to do some research and prepare yourself physically and mentally. Make sure you have the right gear, such as comfortable training clothes and shoes. It's also a good idea to learn some basic Chinese phrases, especially those related to martial arts. This will help you communicate with your instructors and fellow students.

### Culture and Etiquette
China has a rich culture and history, and it's important to respect the local customs and traditions. When training in a Chinese martial arts school, you should follow the rules and regulations of the school. Bow to your instructors and fellow students as a sign of respect. Also, be mindful of your behavior and language, and avoid any actions that may be considered disrespectful.

### Safety
Martial arts training can be physically demanding, and it's important to take safety precautions. Make sure you warm up properly before starting your training, and listen to your body. If you feel any pain or discomfort, stop immediately and consult your instructor. Also, be careful when using weapons, and follow the safety guidelines provided by the school.

### Duration and Cost
The duration and cost of training in China can vary depending on the school and the course. Some schools offer short-term courses for tourists, while others offer long-term training programs for serious students. The cost can also vary depending on the level of instruction and the facilities provided. It's important to do your research and choose a school and course that suits your needs and budget.

## Conclusion
China is a martial arts lover's paradise. Whether you're a beginner or an advanced practitioner, there are many places in China where you can see and train Kung Fu and other martial arts styles. From the famous Shaolin Temple in Henan Province to the Wudang Mountains in Hubei Province and the southern Kung Fu styles in Fujian Province, each destination offers a unique and unforgettable experience. So, pack your bags, put on your training clothes, and get ready to embark on a martial arts adventure in China. And who knows, maybe you'll become the next Bruce Lee or Jet Li.
