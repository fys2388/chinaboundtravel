## China Solo Travel Guide: Safety, Hostels, and Making Friends
## Introduction
If you're planning a trip to China, you're in for an adventure of a lifetime. This vast and diverse country offers a wealth of experiences, from ancient temples and bustling cities to stunning landscapes and delicious cuisine. But traveling solo in China can also be intimidating, especially if you're not familiar with the language, culture, or customs. In this guide, I'll share some tips and tricks to help you stay safe, find great hostels, and make friends while traveling solo in China.

## Safety First
When it comes to traveling solo in China, safety should be your top priority. Here are some tips to help you stay safe:

### Research your destination
Before you travel, research your destination thoroughly. Learn about the local culture, customs, and laws, and be aware of any potential safety risks or scams. For example, did you know that in some parts of China, it's considered rude to make direct eye contact for too long? Or that it's polite to take off your shoes when entering someone's home? By understanding these cultural nuances, you can avoid misunderstandings and potential safety issues.

China is a large country with a rich history and diverse regions. Each area may have its own unique characteristics and safety considerations. For instance, if you're planning to visit a rural area, you might want to be more cautious about wildlife encounters or limited medical facilities. On the other hand, big cities like Shanghai or Beijing may have different challenges such as pickpocketing in crowded areas.

You can find valuable information about your destination by reading travel blogs, guidebooks, and official government websites. Additionally, consider joining travel forums or groups where you can ask questions and get advice from other travelers who have been to China.

### Stay in safe areas
When choosing a place to stay, opt for safe areas with good lighting and plenty of people. Avoid staying in偏僻 areas or areas with a high crime rate. In major cities, look for hostels or hotels in well-populated neighborhoods that are close to public transportation and amenities.

For example, in Shanghai, the Bund area is a popular choice for tourists as it offers a range of accommodation options and is close to many attractions. However, it can also be crowded, so be extra vigilant with your belongings. In Beijing, areas like Wangfujing or Xidan are relatively safe and have a lot of activity.

If you're traveling to a smaller town or village, try to stay in the center where there are more people around. You can also ask locals for recommendations on safe places to stay. They may be able to give you insights based on their own knowledge and experiences.

### Be cautious with your belongings
Keep your valuables safe and avoid carrying large amounts of cash or wearing expensive jewelry. Be especially cautious in crowded places, such as markets and public transportation, where pickpocketing is common. Consider using a money belt or a crossbody bag that you can keep close to your body.

When using ATMs, make sure to cover the keypad with your hand or a piece of paper to prevent anyone from seeing your PIN. Also, be aware of your surroundings and avoid using ATMs in isolated or poorly lit areas.

If you're carrying a backpack, keep it in front of you in crowded places to prevent someone from accessing it from behind. And when you're sitting in a restaurant or a café, don't leave your bag unattended, even for a moment.

### Use common sense
Trust your instincts and use common sense when traveling. If something doesn't feel right, it probably isn't. Avoid walking alone at night, especially in unfamiliar areas, and don't accept rides from strangers.

If you find yourself in a situation where you feel threatened or unsafe, try to stay calm and find a way to get to a safe place. You can also call the local police or emergency services if necessary.

Another important aspect of using common sense is to be aware of the local laws and regulations. For example, in China, it's illegal to take pictures of certain government buildings or military installations. Make sure to respect these laws to avoid any potential problems.

### Learn some basic Chinese
Learning some basic Chinese phrases can go a long way in helping you communicate with locals and stay safe. Some useful phrases include "Ni hao" (hello), "Xie xie" (thank you), "Bu yao" (no), and "Jiu ming" (help).

Even if you only know a few words and phrases, it can show that you're making an effort to communicate and can help build a connection with the locals. You can also use translation apps or dictionaries on your phone to assist you in communicating.

However, keep in mind that not everyone in China speaks English, especially in rural areas. So, it's a good idea to learn some basic Chinese phrases before you travel.

## Finding Great Hostels
Hostels are a great option for solo travelers in China, as they offer a budget-friendly and social way to travel. Here are some tips to help you find great hostels:

### Use hostel booking websites
There are many hostel booking websites available, such as Hostelworld and Booking.com, that allow you to search for hostels in China and read reviews from other travelers. These websites can be a valuable resource for finding hostels that meet your needs and budget.

When using hostel booking websites, make sure to read the reviews carefully. Look for hostels that have good reviews for safety, cleanliness, and location. You can also filter your search results by price, amenities, and other factors to find the excellent hostel for you.

Some hostel booking websites also offer exclusive deals and discounts for their members. So, it's worth signing up for an account to take advantage of these offers.

### Consider the location
When choosing a hostel, consider the location carefully. Look for hostels that are located in convenient areas, close to public transportation, attractions, and amenities.

For example, if you're planning to visit the Terracotta Army in Xi'an, you might want to stay in a hostel that is close to the train station or the bus stop that takes you to the museum. This will save you time and money on transportation.

In addition to being close to attractions, you also want to choose a hostel that is located in a safe and quiet neighborhood. Avoid staying in hostels that are located in areas with a lot of noise or traffic.

### Look for hostels with good amenities
When choosing a hostel, look for hostels that offer good amenities. Some hostels may offer free Wi-Fi, breakfast, laundry facilities, and other amenities.

Free Wi-Fi is essential for most travelers, as it allows you to stay connected with family and friends, research your destination, and book activities. Breakfast is also a nice bonus, as it can save you money and time in the morning.

Laundry facilities can be useful if you're traveling for an extended period of time. And some hostels may also offer other amenities such as a common room, a kitchen, or a rooftop terrace.

### Read the reviews
Before booking a hostel, make sure to read the reviews carefully. Look for hostels that have good reviews for safety, cleanliness, location, and amenities.

Pay attention to the details in the reviews. For example, if a reviewer mentions that the hostel is noisy at night, you might want to consider staying elsewhere. Or if a reviewer mentions that the hostel is not clean, you might want to avoid it.

You can also look for reviews from travelers who have similar interests and travel styles as you. This can give you a better idea of whether the hostel is a good fit for you.

### Consider the price
When choosing a hostel, consider the price carefully. Hostels in China can range in price from very affordable to more expensive.

Set a budget for your accommodation and look for hostels that fit within your budget. However, don't sacrifice quality for price. Make sure to choose a hostel that offers good value for money.

Some hostels may offer discounts for longer stays or for booking in advance. So, it's worth checking the hostel's website or contacting them directly to see if they have any special offers.

## Making Friends
Traveling solo in China can be a great opportunity to meet new people and make friends. Here are some tips to help you make friends while traveling solo in China:

### Stay in hostels
Staying in hostels is a great way to meet other travelers. Most hostels have common rooms where you can relax, watch TV, or play games with other guests.

You can also participate in hostel-organized activities such as pub crawls, city tours, or cooking classes. These activities are a great way to meet other travelers who share your interests and have similar travel styles.

### Join a tour
Joining a tour is another great way to meet new people and make friends. There are many different types of tours available in China, such as city tours, cultural tours, and adventure tours.

When joining a tour, make sure to choose a tour that interests you and that has a good reputation. You can also ask other travelers for recommendations on tours.

During the tour, make an effort to talk to other members of the group. Share your experiences, ask questions, and listen to their stories. This can help you build a connection with other travelers and make new friends.

### Attend local events
Attending local events is a great way to meet new people and experience the local culture. There are many different types of events available in China, such as festivals, concerts, and art exhibitions.

You can find information about local events by checking the local newspapers, magazines, or websites. You can also ask locals for recommendations on events.

When attending an event, make an effort to talk to other people. Strike up a conversation, ask questions, and share your interests. This can help you meet new people and make new friends.

### Use social media
Social media can be a great way to meet new people and make friends while traveling solo in China. You can use social media platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter to connect with other travelers who are in China or who have traveled to China.

You can also join travel groups or forums on social media where you can ask questions, share your experiences, and meet other travelers.

However, be careful when using social media. Don't share too much personal information or reveal your location. And be wary of strangers who may try to contact you.

### Volunteer
Volunteering is a great way to meet new people and make a difference in the world. There are many different types of volunteer opportunities available in China, such as teaching English, working with animals, or helping with community projects.

You can find information about volunteer opportunities by checking the local newspapers, magazines, or websites. You can also ask locals for recommendations on volunteer organizations.

When volunteering, make an effort to talk to other volunteers and the people you're helping. Share your experiences, ask questions, and listen to their stories. This can help you build a connection with other people and make new friends.

## Conclusion
Traveling solo in China can be a rewarding and unforgettable experience. By following these tips and tricks, you can stay safe, find great hostels, and make friends while traveling solo in China.

Remember to research your destination thoroughly, stay in safe areas, be cautious with your belongings, use common sense, and learn some basic Chinese. And don't forget to have fun and enjoy your adventure!
