# 🌐 Internet Access in China

![Global internet connectivity](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1558494949-ef010cbdcc31?w=800&h=400&fit=crop)

Stay connected during your trip to China. Our comprehensive guide covers everything from eSIM options to reliable VPN services.

## Connectivity Options

### eSIM
- Easy activation without physical SIM
- Multiple providers available
- Perfect for short-term visits

### Local SIM Card
- Best value for longer stays
- Data plans starting from ¥30/month
- Available at airports and convenience stores

### Pocket WiFi
- Share connection with travel companions
- Battery-powered portability
- Ideal for group travel

## Tips for Staying Connected

1. Activate your eSIM/SIM before arriving in China
2. Download VPN apps and connect immediately after landing
3. Keep offline maps downloaded for navigation
4. Consider purchasing a local number for WeChat Pay
