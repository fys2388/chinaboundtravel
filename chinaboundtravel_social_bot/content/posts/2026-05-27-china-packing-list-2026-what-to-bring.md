## The Honest Packing List That Will Save You 10 Years of Trial and Error

**By Joran | 50+ China trips and counting**

My first China trip, I brought four pairs of shoes, a sleeping bag liner, a travel iron, and zero toiletries I actually needed. I forgot: a power adapter, enough prescription medication, and any concept of how cold Beijing February actually is.

Here is the packing list I actually use now — updated for 2026 travel to China.

---

## The Non-Negotiables (Do Not Leave Home Without)

### 1. Passport + Copies (Physical + Digital)
Always. Carry a physical copy separate from your passport. Email yourself a scan. Store a photo in your phone.

### 2. VPN — Installed BEFORE Arrival
I use ExpressVPN. Download it, install it, log in, and test it before you board your flight. Once you are in China, you cannot access VPN app stores to download it.

### 3. eSIM or International Data Plan
I use Airalo for China eSIM. Order it before departure, scan the QR code when you land, have data before you exit the terminal.

### 4. Power Adapter — China Type I
China uses Type I sockets (three flat prongs). Most modern laptop chargers are dual-voltage — you just need the physical adapter.

### 5. Portable Charger — 10,000mAh Minimum
Your phone with no navigation and no Alipay is useless. Carry a 10,000-20,000mAh portable charger. Note: lithium batteries must be in carry-on, not checked luggage.

## Weather-Specific Packing

| Season | Essentials | Avoid |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Jan-Feb (Winter) | Thermal base layers, down jacket, hand warmers, moisturizer, lip balm | Cotton (slow to dry), light jackets |
| Mar-Apr (Spring) | Layers, light rain jacket, allergy meds if sensitive | Heavy coats |
| May-Aug (Summer) | Moisture-wicking clothes, DEET repellent, sun hat, shorts | Denim (too hot), formal shoes |
| Sep-Oct (Autumn) | Fleece layer, light down jacket, versatile layers | Heavy winter gear |

---

## The Toiletries Reality Check

You can buy everything in China. BUT specific brands may not exist.

**Bring:** Prescription medications, contact lens solution, sunscreen SPF50+, DEET mosquito repellent for summer/fall.

**Do not bring:** Shampoo, conditioner, basic first aid, toothbrush, toothpaste — available everywhere at Watsons and Mannings.

---

## The 10 Things First-Timers Forget

1. **A small daypack** — For day trips, you do not want your whole bag
2. **Toilet paper/wet wipes** — Not always provided in public restrooms
3. **A reusable water bottle** — Tap water is not potable in China
4. **A money belt** — For passport and backup credit card
5. **Passport photos** — Useful for local services and train ticket upgrades
6. **A silk sleep sack** — Solves the budget hotel sheets problem
7. **Translation app with offline package** — Google Translate offline Chinese
8. **Compression socks** — For long flights and high-speed train rides
9. **A carabiner or small clip** — Surprisingly useful for hostel storage
10. **A sense of humor** — You will need it

## The One Rule

Pack half of what you think you need. Pay for the laundry. Bring twice as much money as you think. And test your VPN before you get on the plane.
