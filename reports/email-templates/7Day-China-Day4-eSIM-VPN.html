Hey Traveler,

Day 4. Let's talk about the Great Firewall — not the tourist one, the digital one. In China, Google, Instagram, WhatsApp, Gmail, and Netflix are all blocked. Your phone will feel like it's been sent back to 2005.

The Two-Part Fix

1. eSIM for data. You need a local data connection. Airport WiFi is unreliable and hotel WiFi often can't handle video calls. An eSIM gives you 4G/5G everywhere.

Get Airalo China eSIM →

2. VPN for access. Even with local data, Google and friends are blocked. You need a VPN that works in China. Not all do — many get blocked the moment you cross the border. I use NordVPN; it has obfuscated servers specifically designed for China.

Get NordVPN for China →

Critical: Install and TEST your VPN before you arrive in China. Once you're behind the firewall, you can't download a VPN app — the App Store won't even show it. Set it up at home, connect to a Hong Kong or Japan server, confirm Google works, then you're ready.

What Actually Works Inside China

  • WeChat: Your everything app — messaging, payments, maps, mini-programs for subway tickets
  • Dianping (大众点评): Like Yelp but actually useful, with restaurant menus and queue systems
  • Amap (高德地图): The best navigation app in China, way more accurate than Google Maps here
  • Apple Music / Spotify (with VPN): Works fine through VPN
