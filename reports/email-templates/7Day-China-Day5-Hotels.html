Hey Traveler,

Day 5. Let's find you a bed. China's hotel scene is wild — you can get a decent private room for $25/night or a 5-star suite for $80. The trick is knowing where to look and what to avoid.

My Booking Strategy

  • Book through Booking.com: Free cancellation on most rooms, English reviews, and you can filter by "foreigners accepted" (some Chinese hotels only take local ID)
  • Location > luxury: Stay near a subway station. A 10-minute walk to the metro beats a fancy hotel that's 45 minutes from everything
  • Check recent reviews (last 3 months): Hotels in China change management fast — a 2023 glowing review might describe a completely different place now
  • Avoid "airport hotels" unless you have a 6 AM flight: They're usually isolated and overpriced

Budget hack: In tier-2 cities (Chengdu, Xi'an, Hangzhou), 4-star hotels regularly go for $35–$50/night. In Beijing and Shanghai, budget $60–$100 for something decent near the center. Hostels are $10–$15/night and great for meeting other travelers.

Tours & Activities

For guided tours, skip the hotel desk (they mark up 30–50%) and book through Klook. They have English-speaking guides, skip-the-line tickets for the Forbidden City and Terracotta Warriors, and prices are transparent.
