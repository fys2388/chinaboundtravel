Hey Traveler,

Day 6. Getting around China is either the best part of your trip or the most stressful, depending on how you plan it. The high-speed rail network is genuinely incredible — 350 km/h, on time, comfortable, and cheaper than you think.

High-Speed Rail (The Star of the Show)

  • Beijing → Shanghai: 4.5 hours, ~$80 second class. Flights take 2.5 hours but add 3 hours of airport hassle
  • Beijing → Xi'an: 4.5 hours, ~$70. Perfect for the Terracotta Warriors day trip
  • Shanghai → Hangzhou: 45 minutes, ~$15. Day trip, no brainer
  • Book through Trip.com app or at the station with your passport. Buy 3–7 days ahead for popular routes

Domestic Flights

For long distances (Chengdu → Beijing, Shanghai → Guilin), flights make sense. Use Aviasales to compare prices across Chinese airlines — they aggregate domestic carriers and often find deals 20–30% cheaper than booking direct.

Booking everything in one place: Trip.com (the international version of Ctrip) handles flights, trains, hotels, and airport transfers in English. It's the app I open most when I'm on the move in China.

City Transport

  • Subway: Clean, fast, cheap ($0.50–$1 per ride). Use WeChat or Alipay to scan through gates
  • Didi (滴滴): China's Uber. Cheaper and more available. English interface available
  • Avoid taxis from the airport queue: They'll try to charge flat rates. Use Didi instead
