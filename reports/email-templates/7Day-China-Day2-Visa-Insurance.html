Hey Traveler,

Day 2. Let's talk visas — the thing that can either be a non-issue or a full-on bureaucracy nightmare, depending on your passport.

The Short Version

  • 144-hour visa-free transit: If you're passing through to a third country, you get 6 days visa-free in certain cities. Game changer for stopovers.
  • 15-day visa-free: Citizens of many countries (France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Malaysia, Thailand, and more) can visit for 15 days without a visa. Check the latest list — it changes often.
  • Everyone else: Standard L (tourist) visa. Apply at your nearest Chinese consulate or via an agency. Budget $150–$200.
