Day 7. The final email. You've got eSIM, visa, payment, internet, hotels, and transport figured out. Now let me hand you the actual itinerary — the one I recommend to friends visiting China for the first time.

The Classic 10-Day Route

Days 1–3: Beijing
Forbidden City (book ahead!), Great Wall at Mutianyu (not Badaling), Summer Palace, Wangfujing food street. Stay near Wangfujing or Qianmen.

Days 4–5: Xi'an
High-speed train from Beijing (4.5h). Terracotta Warriors (go at opening!), Muslim Quarter food, City Wall bike ride. One full day is enough.

Days 6–7: Chengdu
Fly or train from Xi'an. Giant Panda Breeding Center (arrive before 8 AM!), Jinli Ancient Street, hot pot (obviously), People's Park tea house.

Days 8–10: Shanghai
Fly from Chengdu (2.5h). The Bund, Yu Garden, French Concession cafes, day trip to Hangzhou or Suzhou. End with a Huangpu River cruise.

Budget estimate (per person, mid-range): Flights $800–$1200, hotels $50–$80/night × 10 = $500–$800, food $20–$40/day = $200–$400, transport & attractions $300–$500. Total: ~$2,000–$3,000 for 10 days.
